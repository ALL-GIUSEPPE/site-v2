About:

Giuseppe is a mixed media performance artist from Friesland now based in Tilburg, the Netherlands. Giuseppe, also known as Giuso, is firmly gripped by the concept of the absurd. His performances and films explore aspects of his life and the human condition through humour, sincerity, irony and a stream of consciousness. He currently studies ‘art and research’ at S.T.
